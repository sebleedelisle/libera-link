Libera Link: OpenIDN and IDN third-party notices
==============================================

Libera Link's IDN support is based on OpenIDN, including work by DexLogic /
Dirk Apitz and the University of Bonn OpenIDN Laser DAC Framework team
(Matthias Frank, Leander Schickhaus and Christian Windeck), with HeliosPRO
adaptations by Gitle Mikkelsen.

The vendored subset in src/third_party/openidn was imported from:
  https://github.com/sebleedelisle/helios_openidn
  Base revision: 72b7463e22f6d01e2f8a48779f86dbe2aa49d2b0
  Upstream Helios adapter: https://github.com/Grix/helios_openidn

Libera Link includes local modifications to this subset. Standalone server
entrypoints and unused hardware backends are excluded.

The Helios SDK dependency also contains DexLogic-derived IDN client code in
extern/libera-laser/libs/helios_dac/sdk/cpp/idn.

The following notices cover these OpenIDN and IDN components. Other third-party
components retain their own licences; see LICENSING.md and their source files.
